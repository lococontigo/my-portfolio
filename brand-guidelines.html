10 · Voice & Principles
How the brand behaves
Applies to every client touchpoint — site, proposals, invoices, email.
01
Outcome-driven
Every piece of communication moves the reader toward a goal — understanding the work, booking a call, signing off. Cut anything that doesn't.
02
Trust by transparency
Real names, real roles, real links. No placeholder content, ever, in anything client-facing.
03
Micro-interactions, macro impact
Hover and focus states are deliberate, never decorative. HK Night = electric/neon. CA Stone = soft/warm.
04
Motion with intent
Animation communicates state or hierarchy, never decoration for its own sake. Sharp ease-out only.
05
Accessible by default
Visible focus states, sufficient contrast in both worlds, descriptive alt text — non-negotiable.
